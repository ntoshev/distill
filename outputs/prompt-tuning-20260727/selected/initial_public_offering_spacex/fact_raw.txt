SpaceX IPO occurred on June 12, 2026, with a target valuation of US$1.77 trillion.
SpaceX IPO listed on the Nasdaq exchange under the ticker symbol SPCX.
SpaceX IPO raised US$75 billion through the sale of 555,555,555 Class A shares.
SpaceX IPO subscription price was set at US$135 per share before the roadshow.
SpaceX IPO roadshow took place between June 3 and June 11, 2026.
SpaceX IPO first-day close reached US$161 per share, representing a 19% increase.
SpaceX IPO market capitalization approximated US$2.1 trillion on the first trading day.
SpaceX IPO voting control remained with Elon Musk, who held 82–85% of voting power.
SpaceX IPO lock-up period was 366 days for Musk and insiders, and 180 days for other pre-IPO investors.
SpaceX IPO lead underwriters included Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley.
SpaceX IPO included a greenshoe option of 83,333,333 shares, equal to 15% of the issue.
SpaceX IPO index inclusion in the Nasdaq-100 was scheduled for 15 trading days post-IPO.
SpaceX IPO valuation relied heavily on a total addressable market of US$28.5 trillion attributed to artificial intelligence.
SpaceX IPO faced criticism from analysts who described the valuation as significantly overvalued.
SpaceX IPO post-listing bond issue totaled US$25 billion after being oversubscribed.
